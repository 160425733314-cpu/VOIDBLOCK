Controls

Arrow keys or WASD roll the block. Swipe the board or use the on-screen pad on touch screens. R restart · Esc pause · G gates.

Privacy

Progress and settings are saved only in this browser (local storage). There are no accounts, analytics or tracking. The page loads Three.js and a font from public CDNs.
